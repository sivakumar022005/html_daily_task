DC is a 2026 Tamil-language romantic action-thriller directed by Arun Matheswaran and produced by Sun Pictures. It marks acclaimed filmmaker Lokesh Kanagaraj's debut as a lead actor, starring alongside Wamiqa Gabbi and Sanjana Krishnamoorthy. Released on August 7, 2026, the film is a gritty, violent reimagining of the classic novel Devdas, with the title standing for the lead characters Devadas and Chandra. Lokesh Kanagaraj's stony stillness and rugged screen presence as Devadas received considerable attention and positive reviews for a debut lead performance.
